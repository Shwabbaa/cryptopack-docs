Evolutions
=======================
CryptoCraft Evolutions are power-ups you can apply to your items.

To apply an evolution to an item, you need to combine it with a corresponding Evolution Core. There can only be one evolution on one item at a time.

There are 2 ways to get evolutions:

#. Evolution Boxes (Drops Evolution Cores directly)

#. Backrooms (Drops Evolution Stones, that can be crafted into Evolution Cores with 4 Evolution Shards)

.. _evolution-box:
Evolution Boxes
----------------
Evolution Boxes are lootboxes that give a random evolution core.

They can be obtained through the following methods:

#. Unlocking a vault from the vanilla trial chamber or any modded structure:

   * Regular Vault:

      * 30% for vanilla trial chambers

      * 20% for Shrines

      * 25% for any other modded structure

   * Ominous Vault:

      * 65% for vanilla trial chambers

      * 50% for any other modded structure (incl. Shrines)

#. Opening a chest or barrel in some structures:

   * Vanilla Minecraft

      * 100%: Buried Treasure

      * 25%: Ancient City

      * 15%: Trial Chamber

      * 10%: Woodland Mansion, End City

   * `Dungeons and Taverns <https://modrinth.com/datapack/dungeons-and-taverns/gallery>`__

      * 20%: Hamlet (Treasure), Nether Port (Treasure)

      * 15%: Illager Barracks (Tower)

      * 10%: Nether Port (Regular), Piglin Camp, Stray Fort (Regular), Hamlet (Regular), Illager Mansion, Illager Camp, Illager Barracks, Piglin Outstation, Nether Tower, End Castle, End Lighthouse

      * 7.5%: Lone Citadel

      * 5%: Stray Fort (Food), Nether Bridge

      * 2.5%: Every Other Structure

  * `Incendium (Legacy) <https://stardustlabs.miraheze.org/wiki/Incendium#Structures>`__

      * 20%: Sanctum (Rare)

      * 10%: Sanctum (Lower), Forbidden Castle (Non-Food), Every Other Structure

      * 5%: Sanctum (Junk)

      * 2.5%: Forbidden Castle (Food)

The Evolutions
-------------------

Bounty
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Gives you a 5% chance to earn 10x drops while mining ores, stacks multiplicatively with Fortune.

Found in floor: Dark (3)

Items: Pickaxes

Super Multishot
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
For Arrows: Shoots 5 projectiles, or 9 if combined with Multishot.

For Fireworks: Shoots 3 projectiles, or 5 if combined with Multishot.

Found in floor: Dark (3)

Items: Crossbow, Bow (but stacking multishot does not work)

Very Serious Evolution
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Become able to unstrip logs

Found in floor: Garden (1)

Items: Axes

Paledon
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Take 65% less damage and 35% less knockback when standing still

Found in floor: Hallway (0)

Items: Chestplates

Cooldown Cut Down
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
+40% Attack Speed

Found in floor: Hallway (0)

Items: Swords, Katanas, Longswords, Mace, Trident, Spears


Lightning
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Upon Activation, charge up a lightning strike on your next attack which deals 2x damage towards players and 3x damage towards mobs.

Cooldown: 15s

Found in floor: Hallway (0)

Items: Swords, Katanas, Longswords, Mace, Trident, Spears

Fireball
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Upon Activation, launch a Fireball that does not do damage to blocks

Cooldown: 30s

Found in floor: Spirit (-2)

Items: Swords, Katanas, Longswords

Vampire
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Heal over time after killing players or mobs:

* Mobs: 2 hearts over 3.2 seconds

* Players: 5 hearts over 8 seconds

Found in floor: Red (-1)

Items: Swords, Katanas, Longswords, Mace, Spears

Ground Pound
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Launch yourself in the air and deal 15-25 damage to mobs within 5 blocks depending on their distance from your feet.

Cooldown: 30s

Found in floor: Spirit (-2)

Items: Hammers

Sniper
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Arrows travel instantly to your crosshair target without gravity dropoff.

Found in floor: Hallway (0)

Items: Bows, Crossbows

Tree Feller
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Breaks all logs in a tree at once. The tree must have leaves to be recognized.

Found in floor: Garden (1)

Items: Axes

Combo
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Deal more damage with consecutive hits against the same mob.

Found in floor: Dark (3)

Items: Swords (excluding hammers and greatswords)

Parry
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
After blocking a mob's hit, your next attack deals up to 2.5x damage. The bonus depends on how quickly you attack back.

Found in floor: Red (-1)

Items: Shields

Poison Edge
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Attacks poison the target for 5 seconds with Poison II. Undead mobs receive Wither instead.

Found in floor: Spirit (-2)

Items: Swords, Hammers, Spears

Necromancer
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Mobs killed with the weapon become allied skeletons that fight for you. Skeletons disappear after 3 minutes.

Found in floor: Red (-1)

Items: Swords, Hammers, Spears, Mace

Chemical Warfare
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Tipped arrows fired from this crossbow create a lingering effect on impact.

Found in floor: Dark (3)

Items: Crossbows

Rupture
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
After activation, Your next arrow spawns an end crystal that does not break blocks.

Cooldown: 30s

Found in floor: Spirit (-2)

Items: Bows, Crossbows

Master Baiter
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Gain a 33% chance to catch treasure while fishing.

Found in floor: Garden (1)

Items: Fishing Rods

Demolition
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Destroys dirt, sand, snow, and stone in a 3-block radius around the block you are looking at. Ores remain intact, and destroyed blocks do not drop items.

Found in floor: Red (-1)

Items: Pickaxes

Bedrock Smasher
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Allows you to break bedrock, but bedrock does not drop as an item.

Found in floor: Red (-1)

Items: Pickaxes

Chaos
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Shoot random tipped arrows.

Found in floor: Spirit (-2)

Items: Bows, Crossbows

Flingshot
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Ride the arrows you shoot.

Cooldown: 20s

Found in floor: Sewer (2)

Items: Bows, Crossbows

Telekinesis
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Block drops go directly into your inventory and block interaction range increases by 1 block.

Found in floor: Sewer (2)

Items: Helmets

Photosynthesis
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Heal health and up to 9 hunger bars when exposed to light.

Found in floor: Garden (1)

Items: Helmets

Overshield
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Gain up to 3 absorption hearts after avoiding damage for 10 seconds.

Found in floor: Spirit (-2)

Items: Chestplates

Regrowth
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Receive 50% more healing from all sources except other evolutions, including food.

Found in floor: Garden (1)

Items: Chestplates

Deflect
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Take 60% less projectile damage. Enemies that hit you are knocked back.

Found in floor: Spirit (-2)

Items: Chestplates

Razor
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Flying into mobs damages and knocks them back.

Found in floor: Sewer (2)

Items: Elytra

Jetpack
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Sneak while flying to propel yourself without rockets. Flight lasts for 10 seconds and recharges while you are on the ground.

Found in floor: Red (-1)

Items: Elytra

Elytra Chestplate
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
When worn, grants the armor, toughness, and knockback resistance of an unenchanted netherite chestplate.

Found in floor: Red (-1)

Items: Elytra

NoFall
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Prevents fall damage and elytra wall-collision damage.

Found in floor: Hallway (0)

Items: Leggings

Sprint
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Running continuously without jumping gives you a speed boost.

Found in floor: Hallway (0)

Items: Boots

Ninja
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Other players, sculk sensors, and the Warden cannot hear your footsteps.

Found in floor: Dark (3)

Items: Leggings

Step
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Increases your step height to 1.15 blocks.

Found in floor: Sewer (2)

Items: Boots

Drill
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Increases mining speed by 50%.

Found in floor: Sewer (2)

Items: All tools

Backstab
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Deal 50 extra damage when attacking an entity from behind (75 damage on a critical hit). Backstabs have a 5-second cooldown.

Found in floor: Dark (3)

Items: Swords, Hammers

Double Jump
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Gain one extra jump while in the air.

Found in floor: Hallway (0)

Items: Boots

Reach
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Increases block and entity interaction range by 1 block.

Found in floor: Sewer (2)

Items: All tools and weapons

Stab
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Deal 40% more damage to unarmored targets, excluding bosses.

Found in floor: Hallway (0)

Items: Vanilla Swords, Longswords, Mace, Spears, Trident

Smelting Touch
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Automatically smelts mined block drops when using the correct tool.

Found in floor: Garden (1)

Items: Pickaxes, Axes, Shovels, Hoes

Enhanced Enchantments
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Increases the level of existing enchantments on the item by 1, ignoring vanilla level limits. Excludes enchantments with a maximum level of 1. Enchantments must be on the item before applying this evolution.

Found in floor: Dark (3)

Items: All enchantable items

Butcher
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Doubles the drops of most animals.

Found in floor: Sewer (2)

Items: Swords, Hammers

Switch Up
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Increases the damage of your first attack based on how long you have been within 20 blocks of an entity. Moving beyond 20 blocks slowly reduces the multiplier.

Found in floor: Spirit (-2)

Items: Swords, Hammers

Push Back
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Right-click attacks deal significantly more knockback.

Found in floor: Sewer (2)

Items: Spears

Harvest
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Automatically harvests and replants crops in a 3x3 area.

Found in floor: Dark (3)

Items: Hoes

OSynxx's Aura
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Hostile mobs and players who attack you within 5 blocks receive Wither II.

Found in floor: Spirit (-2)

Items: Helmets

Arc Slash
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Always perform a sweeping attack, even when you miss, sprint, or land a critical hit. Also increases sweeping attack range and enchants the item with Sweeping Edge 1 if Sweeping Edge is not on the item yet.

This does not increase sweeping damage, so it is best combined with Sweeping Edge.

Found in floor: Red (-1)

Items: Swords, Hammers

Discord Block Button
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Block with your weapon with blocking power equivalent to a shield.

Found in floor: Garden (1)

Items: Swords, Hammers

Aqua God
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
Grants infinite Conduit Power and Dolphin's Grace, but reduces Depth Strider's effect by 50%.

Found in floor: Garden (1)

Items: Helmets
